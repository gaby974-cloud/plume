Plume

A handwriting notebook for Boox e-ink tablets, with Claude built in.

Write with the stylus as on paper, then ask Claude to transcribe, summarize, structure or continue your notes, or to draw a table, diagram or calendar that you fill in by hand. Answers are placed on the page, in blue, under your writing.

Download Plume (APK)

Latest version and release notes: GitHub releases. Interface in English and French.

Features

  • Low-latency stylus writing on Boox (Onyx pen SDK), pens, highlighter and eraser; scribble over a word to erase it.
  • Notebooks with pages, or a single infinite page.
  • Area and lasso selection: move, recolor, resize, duplicate, delete, or send only that part to Claude.
  • Claude modes: Auto (follows an instruction written on the page, otherwise transcribes), Transcribe, Summarize, Continue, Structure, Diagram, Table to fill in, Calendar, Check, and more.
  • PNG and PDF export; optional automatic PDF copy of each notebook to a "Plume" folder in your Google Drive.

Install

  1. Download the APK on your tablet (or copy it over with USB or BooxDrop).
  2. Open it and allow installing apps from this source when Android asks.
  3. In Plume, open Settings and paste your Anthropic API key (create one at console.anthropic.com).

Good to know

  • Requires Android 11 or later. Built for Boox; tested on a Boox Note Air C. Other Android tablets work, without the low-latency pen.
  • You use your own Anthropic API key. Claude usage is billed by Anthropic per use, separately from any Claude subscription.
  • Your notes stay on your tablet. Plume has no server, no account and no analytics. Details in the privacy policy.
  • Bugs and ideas: open an issue.
