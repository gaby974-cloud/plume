Privacy policy

Plume app · effective October 27, 2026

Plume is an Android app for handwritten notes. It has no server, no user account, no ads and no analytics. The developer receives none of your data.

What stays on your device

  • Your notebooks, pages and strokes are stored only in the app's storage, on your tablet.
  • Your Anthropic API key and your Google Drive access are encrypted with the Android keystore and never leave the device, except to the service they belong to (below).

Claude (Anthropic)

When you tap the Claude button, and only then, Plume sends an image of the page (or of the selected area) and your instruction to the Anthropic API (api.anthropic.com), using your own API key. Anthropic processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you do:

  • Plume requests the openid, email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permissions.
  • Your email address is only used to show the connected account in Plume's settings.
  • drive.file limits Plume to the files it created itself: a "Plume" folder and a PDF copy of each of your notebooks. Plume does not read, list or change any other file in your Drive.
  • These copies go straight from your tablet to your Drive; they do not pass through any other server.
  • Google data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

Plume's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • In Plume: Settings → Disconnect Google Drive revokes access; deleting a notebook also moves its Drive copy to the Drive trash.
  • From your Google account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling Plume erases all notebooks and settings from the tablet. PDF copies already uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them.
